Welcome

Come in, slow down, stay a while

Blush walls, light oak tables, arched mirrors and warm light, all gathered around our cherry-blossom tree. Grab a snack at the K-store corner, study in a quiet pod, play with friends, or catch the sea breeze on the terrace.
