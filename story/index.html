Our story

Born from golden hour

SOLÉA comes from the feeling of the sun settling over the lagoon — warm, unhurried and a little bit golden.

Café exterior

We wanted a place people visit for the coffee, stay for the atmosphere, photograph because it is beautiful, and return to because they love the experience.

Our space blends organic-modern and Mediterranean-coastal design: natural oak, travertine-style stone, cream textured walls, rattan and linen, lit warmly from morning to night.

On the menu: specialty coffee, a full matcha bar, fresh brunch and desserts baked in-house — premium, but always approachable.
